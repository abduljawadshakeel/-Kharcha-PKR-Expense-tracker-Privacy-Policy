Privacy Policy - Monivo – Expense Tracker

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Monivo – Expense Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you enter in the app

  • Expense and income entries
  • Categories, notes, and budget-related data

This data is primarily stored on your device to provide app functionality.

b) Device permissions

Depending on how you use the app, it may request limited device access:

  • Saving receipt images (optional): If you choose to save a transaction receipt image, the app may ask permission to add that image to your device photo library. Monivo does not request broad access to read, browse, or collect your existing photos or videos. We do not use a system photo picker to access your gallery for core features.
  • Sharing exports: You may share statements or receipts using your device’s share sheet. Shared content is only what you choose to export from the app.
  • Notifications (optional): If enabled, the app may send bill reminders and related alerts. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings. Core expense tracking works without saving images to your gallery.

c) Device and diagnostics information

To improve app stability, we may use crash and error monitoring tools (such as Sentry). These tools may collect:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Crash logs and technical diagnostics
  • Anonymous identifiers required for debugging

d) Analytics data

The app may use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage (for example, feature usage and stability). This may include app version, device type, and anonymous usage events. We do not use analytics to read your expense entries from your device.

e) Advertising data

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad providers may collect and process data such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Ad interaction data
  • Approximate location (as provided by ad networks)

Ad data processing is governed by Google and AdMob policies in addition to this policy.

f) Importing a bank statement (optional)

If you choose "Import bank statement", the app reads the PDF, CSV or photo you pick on your device only:

  • The file, its text and any photo are not uploaded and are not sent to us or to any third party. Text recognition for scanned pages and photos uses a model bundled in the app and works offline.
  • A temporary copy of the file (and, for scanned PDFs, page images) is kept in the app's private cache while you review it, and is deleted when you finish, cancel or leave the screen.
  • Nothing from the file is saved until you review the rows and tap Import. Only the transactions you keep are stored, like any other transaction. The original statement is not kept.
  • The app remembers, on your device only, how the columns of a statement were arranged and which category you picked for a merchant name, to save you work next time. You can clear the remembered categories in Settings. These are included in a complete backup, not in a wallet backup.
  • A PDF password you type is used once to open the file and is not stored.
  • The app does not write the contents of your statement to logs or error reports.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide core app features (expense tracking, stats, reminders, exports)
  • Maintain app performance and fix bugs
  • Show ads to support the free app experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited technical data with service providers strictly for app operation, such as:

  • Crash reporting providers
  • Advertising providers

4. Data Retention

Your in-app data remains on your device unless you remove it or uninstall the app. Technical logs collected by third-party services are retained according to their own retention policies.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Choices

You can:

  • Delete app data from within the app (where supported) or by uninstalling the app
  • Decline or revoke photo-library save permission if you do not want receipts saved to your gallery
  • Disable notifications in your device settings
  • Control ad personalization through your device and Google ad settings

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Sentry

Please review their privacy policies for additional details on their data handling.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
